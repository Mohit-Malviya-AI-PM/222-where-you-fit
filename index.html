where you fit.

I've been on 222 for almost a year, and I've been waitlisted too. A "not selected" is usually about the mix. One pool has too many people like me, while a table somewhere else that week can't form because it's missing someone like me. Here's how I'd connect the two, and turn the no into a guaranteed spot.

how it works today

saying yesYou join a selection pool for that experience.
matching222 builds tables of about six from that pool. If no good table includes you, you're not selected.
when you hearAbout a day before. For a sunday noon brunch, the app said "by 1:22pm this saturday."
not selectedYou get a refund if it was paid. Nothing else.

From the outside, a decline isn't really about me. It's about the mix. Sometimes a pool has more people like me than its tables can use. Other times a table is one person short, and everyone waiting on it gets turned away. The app's own wording points at the second one: "we couldn't find compatible members."

Each table that forms picks the person you sent plus the five waiting on them. This counts one check-in each, so it's the floor. The bigger win is 36 fewer people a week hearing no.

a two-week test in new york

  1. Split members who end up not selected into two even groups. One keeps today's flow.
  2. The other gets a guaranteed spot, at most once a month, in a pool that's short of people like them. NYC ops gets the matches list every week.
  3. At day 14, compare the two groups and decide together whether to roll it out.

what I'd build first

  • Week 1: the matches list for ops, from pool data you already have. Members see no change.
  • Week 2: guaranteed spots on the not-selected screen, at most once a month per person.
  • Later: point people to pools that need them before they say yes, so fewer declines happen at all.

what I'd ask you

  • How many declines come from tables one person short, and how many from pools that were too full?
  • Could the matcher treat one person as must-place in a pool that's short of their profile?
  • Do invites already go out based on which pools are short?
